# Slice 56 findings: the engine moves from gemini-2.5-flash to gemini-3.5-flash-lite

## 1. What was built

Google's Vertex AI lifecycle page (read 2026-10-08) lists `gemini-2.5-flash`, the platform's grounded-search engine, as retiring on October 20, 2026. Slice 56 measured the replacements Google lists and switched the default to the one that searched in every answer at the lowest cost:

- `scripts/engine_probe.py` runs the 20 Run 1 prompts once each on `gemini-3.5-flash-lite`, `gemini-3.1-flash-lite` and `gemini-3.8-flash`, interleaved per prompt, spend-guarded (before each try it reserves max(100,000 micros, the largest earlier probe entry) and stops at 3,500,000 micros of probe spend; the $15 project cap still applies through `spend.check`). Its decision rule `pick()` was fixed before the run.
- `PRICES` gains `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` and `gemini-3.1-flash-lite` (per-query grounding, $14 per 1,000 queries); the 2.5 row stays for stored observations.
- `metrics.window_models` and `metrics.comparability`: `brand_deltas` and `competitors._comparable` refuse windows that hold more than one engine model, with the pipeline-version reason still first and NULL models ignored.
- `config.engine_model` is `gemini-3.5-flash-lite`; `obs_cost_estimate_micros` stays 50,000 by the spec's formula; `.env.example` follows. The dev `.env` was changed after the merge on the user's go (R9).

The probe (2026-10-08, results in `eval/results/engine_probe_v0.json`):

| Model | Searched | Mean queries | Mean cost per observation |
|---|---|---|---|
| gemini-3.5-flash-lite | 20 of 20 [0.839, 1.000] | 2.0 | 30,039.5 micros ($0.0300) |

| gemini-3.8-flash | 6 of 20 [0.145, 0.519] | 0.5 | 19,476.15 micros ($0.0195) |

The two flash-lite models tied on searched share with identical intervals, so the cheaper was picked. Recorded probe spend: 2,063,363 micros; project spend after it: $13.30 of the $15 cap.

Effects after the switch, accepted in the spec: brand deltas, the `mention_drop` alert and competitor deltas compare nothing for about 14 days per brand; `/competitors/changes` and `competitor_shift` for about 28 days; tasks done within 14 days either side of the switch get a permanent `not_comparable` impact label; spurious `new_source` alerts are likely for about a week.
